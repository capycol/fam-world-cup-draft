Results

Teams will receive the typical +3, +1, and 0 points for a win, draw, and loss respectively.

Penalty Shootout

If teams are level after 120 minutes, the game will go to a penalty shootout. Both teams will receive +1 points due to finishing the regular play level. The winning team in the shootout will recieve +2 points for a Penalties Win to make up the total of +3 points for a win.

Extra points

Teams will receive an extra +1 point for a Goal or a Clean Sheet. They will receive -1 point for any Red Card a player in their team receives.
